Welcome to MetaForge

MetaForge stands as a powerful metadata conversion and annotation tool, meticulously crafted to streamline and standardize data formats within the dynamic field of metaproteomics. This tool seamlessly converts a variety of data formats, including CSV, PSM, XML, and MGF, into standardized outputs such as SDRF, MzID, and MzML. Going beyond conventional conversion tools, MetaForge enriches datasets with user-friendly features, integrating metadata effortlessly. Its intuitive interface empowers users to incorporate relevant information, transforming datasets not only into standardized formats but also infusing them with comprehensive metadata. This approach enhances the organization, interpretation, and sharing of data from both laboratory and bioinformatic workflows. A notable feature of MetaForge is its ability to annotate existing mzID and mzML files with additional metadata, offering an all-encompassing solution for researchers and practitioners. MetaForge provides an efficient means to manage and annotate data, ultimately streamlining the analytical workflow in the ever-evolving realm of metaproteomics. Experience the next level of data management and annotation with MetaForge.
