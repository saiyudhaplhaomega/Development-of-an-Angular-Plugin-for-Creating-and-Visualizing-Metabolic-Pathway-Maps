Group: Multidimensional Omics Data Analysis

Kompetenzfelder: Metaproteomics, Modellierung, Omics Daten, Software-Entwicklung

The working group Multidimensional Omics Data Analysis is a collaboration with the Leibniz Institute for Analytical Sciences (ISAS). The development of novel omics methods in the last 10-20 years now allows for a detailed analysis and characterization of the pathogenesis of most diseases. However, these methods require complex and rapid bioinformatic workflows to identify and interpret the corresponding genes, proteins, and metabolites in the raw data. Subsequently, prognostic and predictive markers, and possibly new therapeutic targets, can be derived from the multi-omics datasets using biostatistical methods and machine learning algorithms. The challenge lies in integrating individual omics data with each other, with clinical data (diagnoses, analyses, therapy), with information from clinical databases, and with already known publications, and finally visualizing the results. In addition, omics datasets enable the development of mathematical models to describe the disease. These models could be used to test therapeutic approaches or could be translated back to the clinic to support diagnosis and clinical decisions.
